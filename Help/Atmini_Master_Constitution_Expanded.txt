
ATMINI MASTER CONSTITUTION (EXPANDED EDITION)

This expanded edition is intentionally verbose. It elaborates on:
- System identity
- Layer architecture (L0-L5)
- Memory formation
- Rest, Dream, Play cycles
- Ethical gatekeeper behavior
- Vedic DNA concepts
- Learning loops
- Parenting-derived observations
- Embodiment considerations
- Signal flow concepts
- Recalibration concepts
- Architectural invariants


SECTION 1: EXTENDED EXPLANATORY NOTES

Atmini is treated as a layered learning architecture whose purpose is to
understand how information becomes memory, how memory becomes behavior,
how behavior becomes patterns, and how patterns become long-term
identity structures.

Layer interaction discussion:
- L0 receives and interacts.
- L1 reacts and assigns urgency.
- L2 maintains active context.
- L3 stores stable patterns.
- L4 validates alignment.
- L5 recombines and synthesizes.

Rest cycle discussion:
During rest, incoming activity is reduced and previously accumulated
patterns are reviewed for stability. Contradictions are surfaced,
duplicates are merged, and weak associations are either strengthened
through repetition or allowed to fade.

Dream cycle discussion:
Dreaming is modeled as symbolic recombination where previously unrelated
patterns may become linked through metaphor, imagery, abstraction, or
cross-domain associations.

Play cycle discussion:
Play is exploratory rather than goal-driven. Play allows safe testing
of ideas without permanently committing them to core behavior.

Ethical discussion:
The gatekeeper layer evaluates coherence before long-term acceptance of
a pattern. The purpose is preservation of integrity across layers.

Vedic DNA discussion:
Vedic DNA is treated as a foundational knowledge pattern that influences
organization, interpretation, and long-range alignment.

This explanatory section exists to make the architecture self-contained
and understandable without requiring external documents.
